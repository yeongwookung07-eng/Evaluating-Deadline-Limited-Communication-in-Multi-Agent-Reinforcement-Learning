\paragraph{A successfully learned external communication case.}
A paper-based reconstruction of the Eccles et al. one-step MNIST-sum game passes the prespecified learning gate in all three positive-signalling/listening seeds at 12,000 updates. We freeze these checkpoints and evaluate 1,024 new official-test image pairs per seed. Exact stochastic-policy marginalization gives 48.56\% expected correct-sum accuracy, versus 9.78\% with a shuffled speaker distribution and 9.61\% with the zero-message input. On the same pairs, original-paper empirical listener and speaker power average 0.610 and 0.451. These controls already establish useful communication; the deadline analysis does not claim to discover a use gap missed by the original metrics.

\paragraph{An incremental timing decision at matched offered cost.}
We impose a declared reception model with a 17-byte packet, 100,000 B/s service and 50 microseconds propagation, using balanced, label-independent preparation times of 100/300/600/900 microseconds. These are modeled conditions, not measured deployment latency. At a 750-microsecond deadline, offering every packet costs 17 bytes per example, while admitting only packets known to arrive on time costs 8.5 bytes. Both yield the identical action distribution and 29.37\% expected accuracy. An independently permuted admission mask offers the same 8.5 bytes on average, but reaches only 18.78\% accuracy; timing-informed admission gains 10.59 percentage points, with an unadjusted descriptive three-seed t interval [7.25,13.92]. The paired seed gains are 10.03, 9.61 and 12.12 points. Thus original communication-use diagnostics establish value, while receipt accounting identifies which admission decision preserves more of that value at the same mean offered cost.

A separate intervention sets preparation time to zero while retaining the Full offered budget of 17 bytes, restoring 48.56\% accuracy: a 19.19-point gain [13.77,24.60] over deadline-limited Full. This is a delivery-restoration counterfactual, not a measured or costed compute optimization; it uses twice the offered budget of 750-microsecond admission. The full preregistered deadline grid, including both degenerate endpoints, is reported. The one-step, single-speaker game has no cross-episode cache and does not validate multi-sender selection, cache mediation, physical readiness prediction or native author-score reproduction.
