\subsection*{Data availability}
This study used simulated trajectories from Hallway, hidden-source broadcast, SMACv2 Terran and SchedNet Predator--Prey, and MNIST digit pairs for the summation game. The recorded data include policy observations, sender identities, actions and rewards, with delivery timestamps and computation times in the deadline experiments.
